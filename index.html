About

I'm Minhajul Islam, currently working as a Software Engineer 3 @ IQVIA. I enjoy working in a team and playing a leading role in building impactful software.

I work as a full-stack engineer. Currently I'm working on the Ai Assistant web application of IQVIA, building features for our cutting edge life-sciences AI Agents. It includes working on MCP servers, creating and updating tools, streaming agent response to chat in the form of markdown, charts, dynamic forms and dashboard widgets. I also led the Agent Studio team where I largely handled dynamic configuration support for all the AI agents that are used across IQVIA. Collaborating with different teams is an integral part of my work, I work alongside ML Engineers, SRE/DevOps team and with Product Managers to transform ideas into features. The tech stack I work with include .NET Core with Entity Framework, RabbitMQ for event driven architecture, Angular, Python, SQL and NoSQL databases, AWS cloud features like EC2, RDS, S3 etc.

I graduated from Bangladesh University of Engineering & Technology (BUET) after completing my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE). During my undergraduate thesis, I worked under the supervision of Dr. Mohammad Shohrab Hossain focusing on networking and security research.
